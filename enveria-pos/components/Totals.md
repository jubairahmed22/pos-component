# Totals

Subtotal, discounts, tax and the grand total at the foot of the cart rail.

## Use
- Anchored to the bottom of the cart rail directly above the Pay button; it never scrolls away.
- The total is `figure-total` (44px mono) — the one number the cashier reads aloud to the customer.
- Figures are tabular mono, right-aligned, so digits never shift as items are added.

## Props
`subtotal`, `discount`, `tax`, `taxLabel` (default `VAT`), `total`, `items`, `currency`.
