# ChangeDue

The post-cash-payment confirmation: money received and change to hand back.

## Use
- Change due is `figure-hero` (64px) on `primary` so the cashier counts from it without leaning in and the customer can read it too.
- Stays up until the drawer is closed or the next item is scanned; never auto-dismiss on a timer.

## Props
`tendered`, `total` or `change`, `currency`.
