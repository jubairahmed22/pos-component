# ShortcutSheet

The keyboard map for register workstations with a keyboard or programmable POS keyboard.

## Use
- F-keys follow common retail convention: F1–F4 tenders, F5 customer, F6/F7 hold and recall, F8 discount, F9 return, F10 online orders, F12 pay.
- The scanner and number keys always work without focusing a field; `/` jumps to search.
- Shortcuts appear as hints on the matching buttons (see `TenderOptions`) and in this sheet from the help menu.

## Props
`keys` (`[[key, action]]`; defaults to the standard map).
