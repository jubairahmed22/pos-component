# Icon

Line icons drawn for this system: 24px grid, 2px stroke, round caps, `currentColor`.

## Use
- Always beside a word on buttons, tenders, badges and banners; icon-only buttons are allowed only for the quantity stepper and must carry `aria-label`.
- Sizes: 24 in buttons, 28 in tenders, banners and keys, 16–18 in badges.

## Props
`name` (see `Icon.names`), `size`, `label` (makes it announced), `className`.
